# Stage 2: Off the Shelf

You use AI tools as-is – Claude Code, Copilot autocomplete, ChatGPT for one-off questions, grammar checkers. You produce with AI regularly, but you are not measuring impact. No deliberate prompting strategy.

**Example:** Pasting code into ChatGPT to get an explanation or fix a bug.

**Counterfeit:** Without a structured prompt framework, you lean on model opinions and model variation hits you harder than the work itself.

**You have completed Stage 2 when you can produce:**

- **3+ Days/Week for 30 Days**
  - Usage telemetry or self-reported log showing AI tool use on at least 3 days per week for 4 consecutive weeks.
- **5 AI-Assisted Deliverables**
  - Links or references to at least 5 deliverables from the past month that include meaningful AI-assisted content.
- **Before/After Prompt Pairs**
  - At least 3 documented prompt iterations showing the original prompt, the revised prompt, and the measurable output improvement.
