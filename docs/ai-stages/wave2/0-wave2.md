# Wave 2: Process

AI reshapes how work gets done.
**Key shift:** You stop asking "how can AI help me do this step?" and start asking "should this step exist at all?"

## The Tool Wall: Why most people stall between Wave 1 and Wave 2

The shift isn't technical — it's mental.

Wave 1 is using AI as a better search engine.
**Wave 2 requires rethinking how you approach work itself.**

### **Identify Barrier**

"I AM a developer" vs. "I do development." When identity is tied to the activity, AI feels threatening instead of empowering.

### **Trust Barrier**

"It hallucinated once, so I can't trust it." One bad experience at stage 2 prevents people from ever reaching stage 3.

### **Process Barrier**

People optimize their AI prompts but never redesign their workflows. The tool gets better, but the process stays the same.
